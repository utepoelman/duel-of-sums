📖 Duel of Sums Rules

🎯 Objective

Collect all the cards from your opponent! Each player starts with an equal number of cards, and you duel by comparing mathematical calculations.

🃏 The Cards

Each card has two numbers: a top number (red) and a bottom number (blue). The top number is always greater than or equal to the bottom number.

Example: A card with "8" on top and "3" on bottom.

⚔️ How to Play

  1. Choose an operation: On your turn, select + (addition), − (subtraction), or × (multiplication).
  2. Calculate: Both players apply the chosen operation to their top card.
  3. Compare results: The player with the higher result wins both cards!
  4. Next turn: The winner of the duel goes next.

🎮 Game Flow

  • You always start first
  • On your turn: Choose an operation
  • On computer's turn: AI chooses based on difficulty
  • Cards go to the bottom of the winner's pile
  • If results are equal: It's a tie! Both cards are discarded and return to the deck

🏆 Winning

The game ends when one player has all the cards. That player wins the duel!

⚙️ Settings

  • AI Difficulty (4 levels):
    • Level 1: Random operation choice
    • Level 2: Maximizes own score
    • Level 3: Uses statistical probabilities
    • Level 4: Advanced strategic play
  • Number Range: Controls the maximum numbers on cards (0-6, 0-12, or 0-20)
  • Starting Cards: Number of cards each player begins with

💡 Tips

  • Watch your opponent's card count in the visual piles
  • Check the game log to see previous duel results
  • Higher AI levels use more sophisticated strategies!
